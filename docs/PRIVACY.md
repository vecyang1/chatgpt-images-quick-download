# Privacy Policy for ChatGPT Images Quick Download

**Last Updated:** October 19, 2026

## 1. Overview
ChatGPT Images Quick Download is an open-source browser extension designed to help users quickly and conveniently save full-size AI-generated images created during their ChatGPT sessions. We believe in absolute user privacy and data minimization.

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
- We do **not** collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data.
- We do **not** collect browsing history, user account information, conversation text, or chat prompts.
- We do **not** use tracking cookies, analytics SDKs, telemetry, or external fingerprinting libraries.
- We do **not** communicate with any remote servers or third-party APIs. All extension operations occur 100% locally within your browser.

## 3. Permissions Justification
The extension requests only the minimum required permissions to perform its core function:
- **`downloads`**: Used strictly to trigger the local browser download of image files directly to your default Downloads directory when you click a download button or batch download action.
- **`activeTab`**: Used strictly to inject and bind overlay controls (e.g. download button, selection checkbox) on ChatGPT image elements when interacting with the active ChatGPT tab (`https://chatgpt.com/*`).

## 4. Third-Party Services
The extension does not integrate with any third-party advertising or analytics services. All downloads point directly to official ChatGPT/OpenAI image URLs hosted on OpenAI infrastructure (`chatgpt.com`, `oaidalleapiprodscus.blob.core.windows.net`, or CDN proxies).
